## English (en-US)

**App name:** dailywall

**Short description:**

<!-- short:en -->
A new wallpaper every day: English words, tips and quotes. Offline, no ads.
<!-- /short:en -->

**Full description:**

<!-- long:en -->
dailywall gives your phone a new wallpaper every day, made from the things you want to learn or remember.

Pick your interests:
• English words for your level (A1–C1), with meaning and an example sentence
• Software tips
• Motivation
• General knowledge

Every morning a fresh wallpaper is drawn on your phone and set on your lock screen, your home screen or both. You will see it dozens of times a day, so learning happens without any effort.

Learns with you
• Know a word? Tap "I know it" and it will not come back.
• Not sure yet? Tap "Show again" and it comes back after 2, 5 and 10 days.
• Nothing repeats until a category has been fully shown.

Made to be read
• Text stays clear of the lock-screen clock and the home-screen icons.
• Long text shrinks to fit, and the contrast is always high enough to read.
• Three styles: minimal, card and bold type, with backgrounds that change every day.

Private by design
• Works completely offline. The app does not even have internet permission.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your history and favorites stay on your phone.

Also: history and favorites, sharing a wallpaper as an image, Turkish and English, light and dark theme, and support for large system fonts.
<!-- /long:en -->

**Keywords / tags:** wallpaper, daily wallpaper, learn English, vocabulary, English
words, motivation quotes, programming tips, general knowledge, lock screen, offline,
no ads, spaced repetition

**Category:** Education (alternative: Personalization)
